Privacy policy

BB Arena Calendar is a personal tool used only by its author.

What it accesses

With the author's consent it creates and deletes events in the author's own Google Calendar, one per court reservation, and adds the author's chosen guests to those events. It does not read any other calendar data.

What it stores and shares

It stores no Google user data beyond the authorisation token kept on the author's own computer. It sells, shares or transfers no data to anyone. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
